About ABACBS

The peak body for bioinformatics in Australia

ABACBS is the national scientific and professional society for bioinformatics and computational biology. We represent researchers, practitioners, and educators at all career stages — from EMCRs to senior faculty and industry professionals.

We advocate for the field, run the annual national conference, support professional development, and connect our community across Australia and internationally.
